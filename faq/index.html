How should I verify an important result?

Start by checking that the entered date, timezone and location match your source. For school terms, public holidays, tides, travel, employment deadlines or legal dates, compare the result with the responsible authority. Our tools are designed for fast planning and checking, not to replace an official notice.

Why can two date counts look different?

Some counts include both the starting and ending date while others measure the elapsed time between them. Timezones and daylight-saving transitions can also affect time-based comparisons. Each calculator explains the convention it uses; choose the option that matches your real task.

What information is saved?

Most calculations run in the browser. A small browser-storage value records whether optional cookies were accepted or declined. Review the Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy, or use the Cookie settings control to change the choice.

Can I suggest a correction or new tool?

Yes. Send the page address, the value entered, the result shown and a link to an authoritative source where applicable. Do not include passwords, identification documents or confidential calendar information.
