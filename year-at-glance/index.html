Year at a Glance

View all 12 months of any year in one compact calendar.

Why a whole-year view is different from a normal calendar

A monthly calendar is good for detail, but it hides what happens three, six or ten months away. This page puts January through December on one screen so you can see the shape of the year before you start filling it. It is particularly useful when a plan crosses month boundaries or when the spacing between several dates matters more than the details of a single week.

Useful planning examples

You can use the view to compare school terms with annual leave, identify long gaps between appointments, map project phases, space out recurring maintenance, plan a content schedule, or see how birthdays and family events cluster through the year. A small business might use it to mark quarterly reporting periods and major campaigns; a household might use the same layout for trips, school breaks and medical appointments.

Use the weekday pattern, not just the date number

The same date falls on a different weekday from year to year. Seeing all months together helps when an event such as the 1st, 15th or 30th repeatedly lands near weekends. That can matter for payroll, deliveries, recurring meetings and travel. If you need to know the weekday for one exact date, the Day of Week tool gives a focused answer.

Printing and marking up the calendar

The Print button is useful when you want a simple paper planning sheet that can be scribbled on, pinned up or used in a meeting. For a more conventional single-month layout, use the Printable Calendar. This year-at-a-glance view deliberately keeps each month compact so the relationship between months stays visible.

What is and is not included

The grid shows calendar dates; it does not automatically add every public holiday, school term, religious observance or personal event. Those vary by location and individual circumstances. Treat this page as the skeleton of the year, then use the site's holiday and school-date tools to layer in the dates that apply to you.
