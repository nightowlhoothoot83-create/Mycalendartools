Cookie Policy

This policy explains the browser storage used by MyCalendarTools, which choices are optional, and how you can change your preference.

What cookies and local storage are

Cookies are small text records that a website or its service providers can store in a browser. Local storage is a similar browser feature used to remember a setting on the same device. They do not give us access to files elsewhere on your device. Removing browser data may reset your choices.

Essential preference storage

We store a value named cookieConsent after you accept or decline optional cookies. This prevents the consent notice from appearing on every page. The value records the choice, not your name, email address or tool entries. It is needed to respect your preference and is not used to build an advertising profile.

Optional advertising cookies

MyCalendarTools is free and may display advertising supplied by Google AdSense. Advertising scripts are activated only after permission is granted through our consent control. Google and its partners may then use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and limit advertisements. The exact cookies and their duration are controlled by those providers and can vary by region, browser and account settings.

If you decline optional cookies, the preference is saved and advertising features that require consent are not activated by our consent script. The calculators, calendars and reference pages remain available.

Changing your choice

Use the “Cookie settings” button at the lower edge of any page to reopen the notice. You can then accept or decline optional cookies. You can also delete cookies and site data using your browser settings; after deletion, the site will ask for your choice again.

Browser privacy controls, content blockers and private-browsing modes can prevent storage or third-party scripts from working. These controls are managed by your browser and may affect whether advertisements appear.

Information entered into tools

Dates and settings entered into the site’s ordinary calculators are processed in the browser unless a particular page clearly says otherwise. Do not enter confidential information. See the Privacy Policy for broader information about data handling and the Contact page if you have a question.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our tools, advertising setup or legal obligations change. The current version is published at this address so it can be reviewed before making a choice.
