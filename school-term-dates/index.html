About the school term dates finder

View school term start and finish dates for supported locations. Term structures vary between education systems and can change from year to year.

How to use this page

  1. Choose the country and region where available.
  2. Select the school year.
  3. Review each term's start and end dates.
  4. Confirm important dates with the relevant education authority or school.

When this page is useful

Useful for family calendars, attendance planning, travel, childcare and comparing academic terms.

Privacy and accuracy

MyCalendarTools keeps these tools lightweight and, wherever practical, performs calculations in your browser. Calendar rules, public holidays, school dates, astronomical data and external forecast data can change or vary by location, so confirm important decisions with the relevant official source.
