How the South African public-school calendar is structured

The national public-school calendar is organised into terms separated by autumn, winter and spring breaks, followed by the longer summer holiday. The national calendar gives families a reliable planning framework, but school-specific notices can still matter around opening, closing and administrative days.

What the holiday table is useful for

Use the listed breaks to plan family travel, leave, childcare and visits. The winter holiday is usually the longest mid-year break, while the end-of-year summer holiday begins after the final term. If a date is shown as two possible closing days, check the current official calendar and the student's school because the applicable date can depend on the school calendar arrangement.

Public holidays and school breaks are not the same thing

South Africa's public holidays can fall inside a school term or holiday period, but a public holiday list alone does not tell you the complete school schedule. For day-to-day planning, use the school calendar first and then note public holidays that affect work, transport, services or family plans around it.

Real-world planning examples

Parents may use the dates to arrange holiday programmes, coordinate annual leave or book long-distance travel. Families with children at different schools should compare their individual calendars before paying for travel. The same applies when relatives live in another province and a visit depends on both households being free.
