About the school holidays finder

Find school holiday periods for supported countries and regions. School calendars differ by state, territory, nation and sometimes individual authority, so the location matters.

How to use this page

  1. Choose the country and region where available.
  2. Select the relevant school year.
  3. Review the listed term breaks and holiday periods.
  4. Confirm dates with the relevant education authority before booking important travel or care arrangements.

When this page is useful

Useful for family travel, childcare planning, leave requests and comparing school-break periods.

Privacy and accuracy

MyCalendarTools keeps these tools lightweight and, wherever practical, performs calculations in your browser. Calendar rules, public holidays, school dates, astronomical data and external forecast data can change or vary by location, so confirm important decisions with the relevant official source.

Examples for 🏫

Common examples include checking a deadline before booking travel, comparing dates for school or work planning, counting down to a public event, or confirming a date before sharing it with family, students, clients or colleagues.

For best results, enter the exact date shown on your source document and check the displayed result before copying it into a calendar, planner, invoice, roster or reminder. If your result affects official deadlines, school attendance, travel, payroll or legal paperwork, use this page as a fast guide and confirm the final date with the relevant authority.
