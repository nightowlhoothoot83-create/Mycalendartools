Why the jurisdiction is specific

Public holidays are not one universal country-wide list. Australian dates vary by state and territory, the UK has separate bank-holiday schedules, New Zealand also has regional anniversary days, US state holidays can differ from the federal schedule, and Canadian provinces and territories have additional dates. This page therefore labels exactly which schedule it is using instead of pretending one list covers everyone.

How to use this page

  1. Select the jurisdiction that matches the schedule you need.
  2. Read the next listed holiday name, date and day count.
  3. Read the scope note beneath the result.
  4. For employment, payroll, travel or legal decisions, confirm the date with the relevant government authority, especially where regional rules apply.

What the countdown means

The day count is the number of calendar days from today to the listed holiday date. A substitute or observed date is used where that is what the official source publishes. Partial-day holidays, local show days and other regional exceptions may need additional checking.
